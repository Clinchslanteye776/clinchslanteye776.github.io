## 📦 What image-crop-batch Does

image-crop-batch focuses on one job and makes it easy to repeat across many files. The app supports two main ways to crop. The first is cropping to the same box across every image. You do not need to rename files, write scripts, or use a command line. The workflow is designed around folders: choose the folder you want to work with, choose how the crop should work, and let image-crop-batch create the cropped copies.
